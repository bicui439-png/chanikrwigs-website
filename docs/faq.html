Questions & answers

How do I book an appointment?
Book online on our booking page, or call or WhatsApp us at 718-676-6003. Our assistant answers 24 hours a day, so you never wait on hold.
What are your opening hours?
Our hours change with the season and the holidays. The booking page always shows the days and times we're open.
How does Wash & Set drop-off work?
Please drop off your wig 3 business days before your appointment. You can drop it off any time, day or night, through the mail slot in our door. Just attach your name, phone number and instructions.
Which wigs do you carry?
We carry Rina, Naya and Nechami, plus wigs from other companies. Come try them on at a consultation.
What if the color I want isn't in the salon?
We carry a big variety of stock, and we can usually get a color for you even if it isn't in the salon. Just ask.
How does paying for a new wig work?
You put down a deposit to hold your wig. The balance is paid when your wig is cut.
Do you repair wigs?
Yes. Book a repair and tell us what needs fixing, and we'll take care of it.
I'm a kallah. Where do I start?
Start with a consultation. See our kallah page for how it works.
